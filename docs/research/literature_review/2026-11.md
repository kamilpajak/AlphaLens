## Anti-pattern flags

- **Retail order-flow basket:** The 2025 matching-market paper is a theoretical framework with numerical experiments, not a long-horizon, net-return equity test. It does not establish a retail-replicable alpha or address patient retail limit-order execution in listed stocks.[1]
- **LLM 10-K basket:** The AI-risk study is relevant to 10-K disclosure extraction and reports more than 30,000 filings across 7,000 companies, but its approximately five-year window fails the minimum 15-year sample requirement and it does not demonstrate net trading returns, turnover, or multiple-testing control.[2]
- **LLM segment-disclosure paper:** This is an extraction and comparability methodology, not evidence that LLM-derived 10-K information produces investable returns. It therefore risks repeating the closed LLM-researcher paradigm without a properly validated trading design.[4]
- **Cross-asset overlays:** The macro-factor paper discusses commodities and broader asset-class exposures, but the available record does not report a qualifying post-2010 out-of-sample net strategy, public-data replication, turnover, or a multiple-testing correction.[5]
- **Crypto microstructure:** The reported data span 2022–2025 and cover five crypto assets, so the study fails the 15-year-history requirement and is not directly transferable to retail equity order flow.[9]
- **Factor-decay requirement:** No located record demonstrated factor decay with a sample of at least 15 years, post-2020 out-of-sample validation, net returns after trading frictions, turnover below 100% annually, public-data replication, and formal multiple-testing correction.
- **Trigger conditions 2–4:** No candidate established a material improvement in retail convertible-arbitrage access, a regime reversal of value underperformance, or materially cheaper access to OptionMetrics or CBOE DataShop.
