Direction-level; live rollout must MEASURE realized-vs-replay before widening.

## 1. What changes and what deliberately does not

Today a pick enters via three server-side resting Limit+GTD orders. With the flag ON, a pick enters via a **watch → touch → trail → fire** machine: no order rests before the tier level is touched; after the touch the entry follows the falling low and buys the d-bounce. `0`/unset = today's behavior byte-identical. Symmetric separation holds: the trail changes WHEN/HOW a tier fills, never WHICH picks trade (same precedent as the day-1 gap gate, `control_loop.py:3213-3222`).

**Killed en route:** V3-as-briefed ("resting LIMIT amended down") is semantically broken — a buy limit above market fills immediately; below market it fills on the falling knife. Bounce semantics = stop-buy semantics; hence V3′.

**Key false premise corrected:** "1 Hz feed" ≠ 1 Hz decisions. The one non-benign effect of lateness is #G1's overnight exposure — covered by the DayOrder guard.

## 3. Money-critical guards (adversarial findings — NORMATIVE, each ships with its trigger test)

- **G1 (CRITICAL) — gap-through pay-up.** A stop-family BUY becomes MARKET on trigger: an overnight gap-up / halt-reopen / reverse-split fills at the open with NO ceiling — the one place trailing is strictly worse than a limit by construction, unpriced by the replay. Guards: fire-price **ceiling clamp** `trough×(1+d)×(1+eps)` (entry-side sibling of `clamp_reanchor_target` — new pure leaf helper, never overload the exit one). **Achievable ON the native trail (option B), NOT a separate StopLimit** — the 2026-08-13 recon (§4b addendum) proved a `TrailingStopIfTraded` order RETAINS a `StopLimitPrice` field, so ONE combined trailing-LIMIT carries the ratcheting trigger AND the fixed ceiling: on the bounce the server fires a LIMIT capped at `StopLimitPrice`, never a naked market order through a gap. The clamp is implemented as `entry_trail_geometry.compute_trailing_order_geometry` (ceiling = `max(trough×(1+d), trigger) × (1+CEILING_EPS_FRAC)`, eps = 20 bps) passed as `place_trailing_stop(..., ceiling_price=)`; the broker tick-aligns it and rejects a BUY ceiling below the trigger. PLUS **DayOrder-only trail orders** — cancel at close, re-arm the watch next session, so no trailing order ever lives through an overnight/halt gap (pending the §4 auction probe, this is mandatory, not optional).
- **G2 (CRITICAL) — KILL must gate entries.** `_run_live_exits_pass` runs UNGATED by KILL (`control_loop.py:398` vs `:383`) — correct for exits, catastrophic if copied: the entry pass takes `kill` explicitly and no-ops fires/amends/placements under it (dedicated KILL test). KILL-transition additionally cancels working `-entry-` family orders (cancel is ungated); the manual-flatten recipe gains "cancel entry-trail stops".
- **G3 (CRITICAL) — per-tier write-ahead BEFORE the POST.** A crash between fire-POST and journal both (a) re-fires on restart (Saxo's 15s x-request-id dedup is useless across a daemon death) and (b) **bricks the whole drain** — the unjoined WORKING verdict trips the gross cap's fail-closed (`control_loop.py:2737-2745`). Guard: append the bracket record with the deterministic `client_request_id` and `entry_order_id=null` BEFORE the POST (join key known pre-POST; fill the id in after), and on TOUCHED-resume query working orders by the `-entry-` ExternalReference family before placing. This deliberately INVERTS the existing POST-then-journal precedent (`:3168-3170`) — for orders the bot manufactures off its own state, journal-first is the safe direction.
- **G4 (CRITICAL) — the startup compactor EATS unknown journal kinds** (`_compact_standalone_stop_journal_lines`, `control_loop.py:2095-2119`, runs every boot). Decision: trail state lives in a **new per-env journal `entry_trails.jsonl`** (sibling of `standalone_stops.jsonl`, latest-marker fold, its own compaction whitelist + growth bound from day one, round-trip compaction test per kind).
- **G5 (HIGH) — invisible reservations + the mid-tick conversion race.** Watching tiers fold to ZERO in `_committed_working_gross_acct`/cash floor → double-spend. Guards: a sibling fold over the watch records (valued at tier LIMIT — conservative-high) added to gross cap and cash floor; atomic handoff = the tier leaves the virtual fold on the exact journal line that creates its bracket record; swap `_place_pick`'s read order to verdicts-THEN-positions (`:3547-3550`) so a fill landing mid-snapshot counts twice (conservative refusal) instead of zero times. NOTE: the cash floor is inert outside declared mode (`:2811-2813`) — the virtual fold in the GROSS cap is what must carry both modes. **Capacity (zen CRITICAL-1 correction):** do NOT fold watching tiers into `open_bracket_count` — with per-tier counting and MAX_OPEN=1 that renders every state of a 3-tier trailing pick illegal and the feature can never arm. Watch capacity gets its OWN pick-denominated limit (`watching picks ≤ 1` initially); the money folds above are what actually protect the account, and the pre-existing MAX_OPEN per-tier-counting re-denomination debt (path-B note in the sizing memo) is called out, not silently papered over.
- **G6 (MED) — cancel-then-verify.** Never write a terminal `expired`/`cancelled` marker from a clock or a cancel ACK while an order id is outstanding — re-read the order; "filled-during-cancel" becomes `fired`.
- **G7 (MED-HIGH) — cash floor re-run at every fire instant.** Tiers fire days apart; tier-3 can fire into an account tier-1 drained (+ a week of USDPLN drift vs the watch-open fx). Insufficient funds at fire → terminal-refuse the TIER (classified via `_is_insufficient_funds`), never leave it re-armed to spam retries.
- **G8 (MED) — partial fills.** StopLimit residual after the price runs = a marketable falling-knife order at residual size — cancel the residual on a short timeout; journal realized qty on `fired` (measurement + reservation release use FILLED qty).
- **G9 (MED, REWORKED per zen HIGH-5) — deep-decline suspend.** The original X≈2-3% conflated two axes (the replay's d is a BOUNCE threshold, not a DECLINE depth — a 3% dip bouncing 0.5% is a VALID best-cohort entry the old rule would have wrongly suspended). Corrected rule: suspend a tier only on evidence-free depth — `trough < NEXT tier's limit` (the ladder itself already encodes "deeper = new tier's job"; E1's watch suspends when price reaches E2, whose own watch takes over) plus a hard LULD/halt gate (no fires on the first fresh tick after a staleness gap > 5 min). Suspended = terminal for that tier, releases its virtual reservation, journals `suspended`; coherent with the ladder by construction. |
- **G10 (LOW-MED) — throttle yield.** Entry amends ride the shared 0.5s client throttle with exits and protection; a violent hour generates ~16-75 amends. Per-uic min-amend-interval ≥5s + coarse step (≥ max(1 tick, ~10% of d)) + hard cap on entry REST calls per tick so protection/exit calls never queue behind entry chatter.

## 5. State machine and journals (D-decisions)

Per tier: `WATCHING → TOUCHED → TRAIL_ARMED → (FILLED | EXPIRED | SUSPENDED | CANCELLED)`. Pick converts armed→watching through the UNCHANGED `_place_pick` gate order (day-1 gate → safety → size → fee floor → gross cap → cash floor); only the tail differs.

- **Journals:** pick-level write-ahead note on `submissions.jsonl` (retires the pick from the drain exactly as today, `est_round_trip_fee_bps` stamped — the estimate keeps pricing off the tier limit, a documented ≤~1% conservative-high proxy); per-tier state in the NEW `entry_trails.jsonl` (`watch_open{limit,qty,d_bps,window_end,fx_rate,crid}`, `touched`, `trough` (≤1 line per tick, NOT per stream tick), `trail_armed{order_id,trigger}`, `fired{realized_qty}`, `expired`, `suspended`, `cancelled`); at fire-arm ALSO the normal per-tier bracket record (G3 ordering) + the `planned` disaster-SL/TP line at FIRE-ARM (not fill) so a fill during daemon downtime is covered by the existing protection pass with zero new code.
- **Trough persistence:** unlike the exit-side peak tracker (in-memory by design — restart reseeds DOWNWARD, safe), a trough reseed is unsafe in the entry direction; restart resumes `trough = min(journaled trough, first fresh price)` — worst case forgets ≤1 tick-cadence of low, conservative.
- **TTL (one rule):** `window_end = advance_trading_sessions(watch_open_date, DEFAULT_ORDER_TTL_DAYS)` journaled at watch-open; every derived broker order gets GTD ≤ `window_end` (never "+7d from its own placement" — no window extension); intraday orders are DayOrder per G1; bot expiry uses the same session boundary, with G6's verify-before-terminal.
- **Overnight re-arm (zen CRITICAL-2 — decided):** after a DayOrder cancel at close, the next session re-arms ONLY through the normal watch-open path: the trough CARRIES (the replay tracked the running low across the whole multi-day window — resetting would silently change the tested policy), BUT fire-arm is blocked until an open-check passes — if the session opens at/below the carried trigger (`trough×(1+d)`), no order is placed that session until a FRESH bounce forms off a new post-open low (trough continues ratcheting; the stale trigger is never handed to the broker into a gap). Fill-during-close-cancel follows G6 (verify before terminal; a fill becomes `fired`, and the re-arm dedup checks the `-entry-` order family first per G3). Honest divergence note: DayOrder + open-check is a deliberate departure from the replayed policy (which held virtual orders overnight) — it trades the unbounded gap-fill tail for a small measured-later fill-rate loss; the measurement join (§5) quantifies it.
- **Day-1 gap gate composes at watch-open:** gap-through-open ⇒ no watch on day 1 (no trough tracked through a falling-knife day); open ≥ E1 ⇒ day-1 intraday touches get the bounce — exactly the +0.025R cohort. Never re-gate the fire.
- **Feed:** the shared INC-2 stream only (`get_shared_price_stream` singleton; the LIVE daemon is the sole elevated-session holder — pre-position subscribe via `live_uic_for`); `is_fresh ≤3s` + `delayed_by_minutes==0` veto = no watch progress on any doubt.
- **Request-id namespace:** parallel `-entry-` family (`<crid>-entry-fire-<n>`, `-entry-amend-<seq>`) so entry and exit ids can never collide on one uic; journal-backed monotonic seq per the existing pattern.
- **Measurement (first-class):** per tier at terminal: `tier_limit` (= replay-implied variant-A entry), touch price/ts, final trough, trigger, order id, realized `avg_price`/qty/ts from reconcile; offline research join (exec_quality pattern, join by order id) → concession, implied ΔR, fill-rate loss. `entry_mode` joins the execution-config version sweep so cohorts never pool across modes (T8).
